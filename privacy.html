Privacy Policy — Rclone Personal

Rclone Personal is a private, non-commercial configuration of the open-source Rclone application.

Google Drive Access

The application requests read-only access to Google Drive to allow its owner to list and download files, including files shared with the owner's account.

Data Handling

Files are transferred directly between Google Drive and the owner's local computer. The application does not operate a separate server for collecting or storing Google Drive files.

Data Sharing

The owner does not use this application to sell, distribute, or share Google account information with advertisers or other third parties.

Access Revocation

Google account access may be revoked at any time through Google Account security settings.
